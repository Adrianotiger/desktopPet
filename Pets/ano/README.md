# Ano Nym (Active)

**Ano Nym** is the fictional character of **My Coding Zone**, the German developer community on Twitch - a mysterious ethical hacker in a white mask and a red bathrobe. He stands for justice, security and passion in the digital era.

Created by **Gregor Biswanger** (Microsoft MVP for Azure AI, generative AI consultant and trainer). The animations are cut from the official My Coding Zone intro video.

## Features
* Walks and runs across the taskbar - sometimes with a burger in his hand
* Trips, falls flat, lies there for a moment and gets up again
* Drops in from the top of the screen on a rope
* Rides an office chair, hacks on the toilet, types at his desk, cheers
* Looks puzzled with a question mark when something is odd
* Two Anos meeting: they look at each other puzzled, argue - and end up in a brawl cloud, exactly like in the intro video
* Reacts to dragging, tossing and falling (own fall pose from the intro)
* Standing height about 100 px (104 x 104 px cells).
