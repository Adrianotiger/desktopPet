# Mago the Wizard

## Description

A kindly old pixel wizard with a long white beard, bushy eyebrows and a blue
robe and pointed hat covered in stars.

He strolls along your taskbar, casts sparkly spells, reads his spellbook,
gazes into a crystal ball, levitates, naps when left alone and sometimes
teleports to another spot in a puff of sparkles.

## About pet

Author: Luan Felipe

Original pixel art, drawn from scratch for desktopPet. Original character —
feel free to distribute.

## Animations

* walk, turn, jump onto windows, fall (soft and hard landings)
* climb the screen border, sit and sleep on the taskbar
* blink, look around, wave, think (strokes his beard)
* cast a spell, teleport (poof out / poof in), levitate
* read the spellbook, gaze into the crystal ball
* drag and toss with the mouse
* the arcane fireball: a circle of runes lights up under his shoes, the fireball
  flies across the screen in an arc leaving sparks, and bursts on the taskbar, a
  window or the edge of the screen (flash, fire, a shockwave of rings that spreads
  over four windows, flying stars, violet smoke, a glowing scorch mark)
* more spells and scenes, many of them with separate little windows: lightning,
  snowman, baby dragon, phoenix, bats, butterflies, dove, whirlwind, magic garden,
  geyser, ice block, storm cloud, magic carpet, magic mirror, hourglass, magic
  letter, enchanted broom, floating candles, tea time, card trick, gold coins,
  flying books, smoke rings, pumpkin, long beard, star catcher, runes, cookies,
  sleepwalk, magic springs, old man walk, knitting, star fishing, hopscotch,
  sparkle hiccups, portal, spirit friend, exercise, message bottle
